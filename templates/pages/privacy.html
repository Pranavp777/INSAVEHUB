Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026. This Privacy Policy outlines how INSTASAVE HUB collects, uses, protects, and discloses information gathered from visitors and users of our website.

1. Introduction & Commitment

INSTASAVE HUB ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website located at instasavehub.com. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information, as well as the choices you have associated with that data.

By using INSTASAVE HUB, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any terms of this policy, please do not access or use our services.

2. Zero-Retention Media Architecture

INSTASAVE HUB operates as a real-time pass-through proxy utility. We emphasize the following privacy guarantees:

  • No Instagram Credentials: We never request, collect, or store your Instagram password, authentication tokens, or personal account credentials.
  • No Media Archiving: We do not store, archive, or retain copies of the photographs, Reels, videos, or audio streams you inspect or download through our service. All media is fetched directly from public Instagram CDN servers and streamed on-the-fly to your local browser.
  • No Content Association: We do not build profiles linking your identity or IP address to the content identifiers or media URLs you analyze.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Disclosures

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements to support the operation and free availability of INSTASAVE HUB. Google and other third-party advertising vendors adhere to strict privacy practices:

  • Third-Party Vendor Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings . Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info .
  • Google AdSense Program Policies: We strictly comply with Google AdSense program policies. Advertisements are clearly separated from download controls, are never disguised, and do not trigger unexpected or automated clicks.

4. Information We Collect

When you visit INSTASAVE HUB, our servers automatically record certain standard non-personally identifiable technical information ("Log Data"):

  • Browser & Device Information: Browser type, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings to optimize interface rendering.
  • IP Address Hashes: For abuse prevention, rate-limiting, and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) mitigation, we process truncated cryptographic hashes of client IP addresses. Raw IP addresses are not stored in application logs.
  • Contact Inquiries: When you voluntarily reach out via our contact form or email, we receive your name, email address, and inquiry message strictly to respond to you.

5. Cookies & Local Web Storage

Cookies are small data files stored on your browser. INSTASAVE HUB uses cookies and local web storage strictly for essential and functional purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: CSRF protection cookies (csrftoken) and session identifiers to secure form submissions and maintain active application state.
  • Access State Cookies: Storing the countdown timer state and free 24-hour access status across page reloads.
  • Advertising Cookies: Cookies set by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to deliver and measure advertisements as described in Section 3.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept essential cookies, some parts of the service may not function properly.
