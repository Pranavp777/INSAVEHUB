Last updated: October 1, 2026. Official Notice & Takedown Procedure in compliance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

1. Overview & Intellectual Property Respect

INSTASAVE HUB respects the intellectual property rights of content creators, photographers, videographers, artists, and rights holders worldwide. We comply with the provisions of Title 17, United States Code, Section 512(c)(3) ("Digital Millennium Copyright Act" or "DMCA") and international copyright treaties.

Technical Nature of INSTASAVE HUB

INSTASAVE HUB does NOT host, upload, store, archive, or cache any user-requested media files on our servers. All video and photo streams are fetched in real-time on-demand directly from Instagram's publicly accessible content delivery networks (CDNs) to the user's browser without permanent server retention.

2. Designated Copyright Agent

If you believe that your copyrighted work is being infringed or accessed through our website without authorization, please direct all formal written notifications to our Designated Copyright Agent:

Designated Agent: Copyright & DMCA Compliance Department

Organization: INSTASAVE HUB Legal Operations

Email: dmca@insavehub.com (or support@insavehub.com)

Response Time: Notices are acknowledged and processed within 24 to 48 business hours.

3. Required Notification Elements

To ensure prompt processing under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), your written notice must include all of the following elements:

  • Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple works are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works.
  • Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, including specific Instagram URLs or INSTASAVE HUB coordinates sufficient to locate the material.
  • Contact information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as your full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and email address.
  • Good Faith Statement: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • Accuracy & Perjury Statement: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

4. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that your access or content was mistakenly flagged or removed due to misidentification or clerical error, you may file a written counter-notification pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3). The counter-notification must contain:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the material that was removed or to which access was disabled.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  • Your name, address, phone number, and a statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the federal district court in which your address is located (or where our provider is located if outside the US).

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable intellectual property laws, INSTASAVE HUB maintains a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, access privileges for users or IP addresses that repeatedly infringe third-party copyrights.
